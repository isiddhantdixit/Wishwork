About the Franchise

The Chennai Super Kings (CSK) are a professional T20 cricket franchise based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. They were one of the original eight teams when the Indian Premier League (IPL) started in 2008. CSK play their home games at the iconic M.A. Chidambaram Stadium, also known as Chepauk.

Over the years, CSK have built a reputation for consistency, calmness under pressure, and strong team culture. With multiple trophies and legendary players, the franchise has become a symbol of loyalty and passion for fans across the world.
